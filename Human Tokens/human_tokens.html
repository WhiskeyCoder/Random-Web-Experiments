⚏ best bursts

    how it works · every keystroke your text is re-split into token-sized chunks by a fast character-count heuristic (≈ one token per 4 characters of a word, short words are one token, CJK is one token per character, emoji cost two) — the same shape real BPE tokenizers produce, without the 2 MB vocabulary. Each chunk gets its own colour so you can see the text the way a model "reads" it. The big number is your live tokens-per-second over the last ~1.6 s of typing; below it your session average, your best burst, and a comparison against real hardware.
    note · hardware tok/s figures are order-of-magnitude community benchmarks (batch=1, typical fp16/quantised setups) — great for comedy, inadmissible in court.
